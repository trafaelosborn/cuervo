# Source archive guide

The full source archive remains with the author's local Cuervo project. It includes census reels, newspaper scans, archival finding aids, transport records, school records, and property documents. Bulk files have not been uploaded to this repository.

- [Source inventory](source-inventory.csv) records project-relative paths, file sizes, and filesystem modification times. Modification times are not publication dates.
- [Original source links](source-links.csv) collects URLs recorded in the included research and manuscript files, with the documents that cite them. Availability and permissions remain those of the originating repositories.
- [The bibliography](../manuscript/book-revision-10/bibliography.md) and chapter notes provide the reader-facing source descriptions and locators.
- Figure provenance is recorded in `sources.json` files under [the figure directories](../manuscript/figures/). Many entries include source hashes, page references, and crop coordinates.

Paths beginning `sources/` or `Cuervo/` refer to holdings in the original project archive. Historical absolute paths beginning with the author's drive letters are provenance records, not links to an online storage service. Original camera files remain in the author's photographic archive; the reproduction assets used by the book are included.

Source excerpts document the book's evidence and support its production workflow. Their inclusion does not apply a new license to third-party material.
