# Cuervo: voice and compression revision

**September 28, 2026 - revision 2**

## Editorial direction

The author asked for Axios editing, a stronger Economist-informed institutional voice and greater confidence. This pass applies the existing Meridian house style across all eight chapters: lead with the consequential finding, explain the mechanism plainly, and keep qualifications where they change the claim.

The [revised manuscript](B:/Meridian/Cuervo/manuscript/book-revision-02/cuervo-complete-manuscript.md) and [revised reading proof](B:/Meridian/Cuervo/output/pdf/cuervo-complete-reading-proof-revision-02.pdf) are a separate editorial version. [Revision 1](B:/Meridian/Cuervo/manuscript/book-revision-01/cuervo-complete-manuscript.md) and its PDF remain preserved. The new pass does not complete all the work identified in the whole-book assessment.

## What changed

- Rewrote or removed 340 original prose blocks. Findings and actors now lead more paragraphs. Repeated explanations of a finding's relevance or limitations have been consolidated.
- Shortened the introductory resolution of the whole argument. Hicks's fuller case remains in Chapter 4; the introductory account gives her financial and political reasons together.
- Retained the detailed commercial, institutional, census and transport evidence while reducing generic commentary around it.
- Made Chapter 7 distinguish documented events, the historical synthesis and conditional mechanisms more explicitly. It now contains 4,489 main-text words, against 6,438 in revision 1.
- Reduced the second discussion of household cooling to a brief cross-reference and conclusion.
- Corrected the claim about an indivisible ninth-grade course. The revised explanation concerns a minimum practical staffing requirement and the options for reorganizing instruction.
- Made the shift from early company accounts to later county agriculture explicit.
- Replaced the suggestion of a generally measured relocation of offices, inventories and services with a claim tied to particular outside connections. The local share of ranch income remains unmeasured.
- Reduced repeated cautions about photographs and family histories in Chapter 8; its ending now states the two outcomes explained by the book.

All 174 source-note definitions and the order of all 220 note references remain unchanged. No new historical sources or empirical estimates were added. Claims about an announced replacement or proposed fund retain their implementation limits; census geography, the distinction between railway administration and shipments, and the uncertainty about later causal shares remain explicit.

## Length: an unresolved publication choice

**The revised body contains 31,494 words, a reduction of 9,763 words (23.7%).** Headings, captions and source notes remain outside this count. This is below the original 40,000-60,000-word ambition and must not be described as satisfying it.

The reduction is mainly interpretation, repeated qualifications and recapitulation around retained evidence. The shorter version gives the author a concrete reading text for assessing the stronger voice. It does not establish that the project lacks material for a longer book. Any expansion should earn its space through historical development, orientation or scholarly comparison. Reinstating repetition solely to reach a number would work against this edit.

## Remaining work

The voice pass advances the substantive revision identified in the [whole-book assessment](B:/Meridian/Cuervo/manuscript/book-revision-01/whole-book-assessment-2026-09-28.md). It leaves distinct tasks:

1. Judge the compressed version's sustained reading experience and settle its length through useful material rather than repetition.
2. Add regional orientation and a cross-chapter functional chronology from held evidence; strengthen the specific engagement with the closest scholarship.
3. Convert working source notes into publication apparatus and complete claim-level verification, including quotations, dates, numbers and locators.
4. Obtain outside criticism and respond; finish the photographic sequence, facing-spread design, cover and physical proof.

No exhaustive business-closure sequence, migration reconstruction, last cattle loading or universal irreversibility threshold has become a prerequisite.
