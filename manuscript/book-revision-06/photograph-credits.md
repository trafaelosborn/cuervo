# Photograph credits

All eighteen photographs are by Taylor Osborn. The sequence moves from buildings and their settings to entrances, interiors, road relationships and surviving fabric. It accompanies the documentary history without assigning unidentified rooms to named residents or inferring closure dates from condition.

Photographs retain their full frames and aspect ratios, with orientation corrected and reproduction resizing only. Exact capture dates are unassigned because archive-folder labels and embedded camera dates conflict. The original identifiers below connect each published view to the photographic collection; metadata and source hashes are retained in the project concordance.
