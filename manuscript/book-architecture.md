## Where the additional substance comes from

- Give changes their historical sequence and explain what each observation establishes.
- Explain the actual organization of trade, finance and institutions before drawing economic implications.
- Use selected census evidence to describe the working community and examine assumptions about age, population and mobility.
- Develop Santa Rosa and Tucumcari only to the extent needed to understand Cuervo's choices and connections.
- Compare plausible explanations, including evidence of recovery, persistence and replacement that constrains a simple collapse story.
- Establish the book's contribution in relation to existing scholarship. Introduce theory where it clarifies a historical problem, then synthesize across cases.

The additional length should not come from a general textbook survey, every archival anecdote, repetitive caveats, long quotations or unsupported scenes. Sparse evidence remains sparse when paraphrased at length.

## A beautiful book as a design requirement

Text and images deserve equal editorial care; this does not prescribe equal page counts. The physical book should be designed around sustained reading and a deliberate visual sequence.

**User-supplied visual reference:** [AlabasterMidwife.pdf](<B:/Space Ranger Press/Books/Supplements/Allies, Adversaries, and Ambiguities/Adversaries/The Alabaster Midwife/AlabasterMidwife.pdf>), visually reviewed across all eight pages. The requested direction is a posh 1990s editorial sensibility. Carry forward high-contrast serif display typography, expressive italics, geometric sans-serif labels, generous margins, restrained blue/gray accents, fine rules and deliberate image hierarchy. Adapt the short-feature layouts to sustained chapter reading: test comfortable text measures, control justified spacing, give captions adequate contrast and establish a rhythm between prose and photographic pages. The reference is a design direction, not a fixed page-by-page template for the entire monograph. The Chapter 1 reading proof is the first Cuervo layout prototype; final typography, image sequence and production specifications remain open for review.

- Develop typography, page proportions, margins and image treatment together using representative spreads before fixing trim or page count.
- Let some photographs occupy a full page or spread where their content and resolution justify it; use quieter pages and intervals to give the sequence rhythm.
- Pair wider landscape views with selected details, avoiding repeated ruin images that imply no activity survives beyond the frame.
- Include maps, restrained charts and selected historical documents when they make geography, change or provenance easier to understand.
- Keep prose pages comfortable for extended reading. Place detailed scholarly apparatus where it remains accessible without overwhelming the main narrative.
- Give captions their own factual review. A beautiful image does not establish its building's historical identity or the cause of damage.
- Review original image files before making reproduction promises. The existing 73-image bank has had a contact-sheet review, not a completed full-resolution print assessment.

The present 12–18-image shortlist is an initial selection exercise, not a final cap on the book's illustrations. **September 27 user direction:** additional source reproductions and newly generated charts and graphs are welcome.
