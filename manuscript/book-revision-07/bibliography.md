# Sources and bibliography

Chapter notes identify the particular pages, newspaper issues, census tables and calculations supporting the text. This list consolidates the works and record series cited there. Original company ledgers, correspondence, conveyance deeds and administrative files known only through later accounts are not presented as independently consulted sources.

## Newspapers, business records and local documents

*Cuervo Clipper*. Selected issues, 1910–1922. University of New Mexico, Center for Southwest Research and Special Collections, digitized newspaper collection. [Collection](https://digitalrepository.unm.edu/cuervo_clipper_news/). Issue dates and page locators appear in the chapter notes. Katharine A. Hicks’s “To My Clipper Readers,” December 1, 1922, is cited separately in the text.

Grubbs, Frank H. *Frank Bond: Gentleman Sheepherder of Northern New Mexico, 1883–1915*. MBA thesis, University of New Mexico, 1958. [Repository](https://digitalrepository.unm.edu/anderson_etds/11/).

Guadalupe County. *City of Cuervo*. Undated parcel map with later ownership annotations. Copy in the author’s collection.

Guadalupe County. Cuervo townsite, Block 20, lots 8–12, assessor property-card packet. January 1983, with December 15, 2008 annotation. Copy in the author’s collection.

Guadalupe County. Survey C-232, school parcel, December 2010; land-exchange surveys C-286(1), C-286(2) and C-286(3), August 2013; survey C-292(2), July 2014. Copies in the author’s collection.

*New Mexico State Business Directory*. 1920 and 1921 editions. Cuervo entries.

Sanborn Map Company. *Santa Rosa, New Mexico*. December 1908, sheets 1–2. Library of Congress, Geography and Map Division, Sanborn Maps collection. [Map record](https://www.loc.gov/item/sanborn05712_001/).

*State Line Tribune*. January 2, 1947. “Modern Feed Milling Plant Big Industry in Curry County” and El Rancho Milling advertisement. Southwest Collection/Special Collections Library, Texas Tech University. [Issue scan](https://newspapers.swco.ttu.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/b7594ff9-73d9-44dc-bc81-8fa4829d50a3/content).

## Population, agriculture, prices and public records

Cuervo locality pages and precinct schedules; 1950 Santa Rosa municipal enumeration districts 10-8, 10-9 and 10-10. National Archives microfilm and digital images. Reels, sheets, lines and transcription limits appear in Chapter 5.

U.S. Bureau of the Census. Published New Mexico population reports, 1910, 1920, 1930 and 1950; *Number of Inhabitants*, 1970; population tables, 1980; *General Population Characteristics*, 1990; *Summary Population and Housing Characteristics*, 2000; Demographic Profile, 2010; Demographic and Housing Characteristics data, 2020. U.S. Census Bureau. TIGER2010BLKPOPHU and TIGERweb 2020 block geometry; 2020 DHC block counts, Guadalupe County. Author’s fixed-window calculations. See also [guidance on interpreting 2020 DHC data](https://www.census.gov/library/publications/2024/dec/c2020br-08.html).

U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. [Historical annual CPI-U table, December 2023](https://www.bls.gov/cpi/tables/supplemental-files/historical-cpi-u-202312.pdf); [“Purchasing Power and Constant Dollars”](https://www.bls.gov/cpi/factsheets/purchasing-power-constant-dollars.htm).

Federal Reserve Bank of Minneapolis. [“Consumer Price Index, 1800–”](https://www.minneapolisfed.org/about-us/monetary-policy/inflation-calculator/consumer-price-index-1800-). Annual historical series, accessed September 29, 2026.

*Burguete v. G. W. Bond & Bro. Mercantile Co.* 43 N.M. 97, 85 P.2d 749. New Mexico Supreme Court, December 20, 1938. [Opinion](https://law.justia.com/cases/new-mexico/supreme-court/1938/43-nm-97.html).
