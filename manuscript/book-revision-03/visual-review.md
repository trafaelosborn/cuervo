# Cuervo: whole-book visual review

September 28, 2026. Revision 3 follows the voice and compression edit.

## Editorial finding

Original graphics improve this book most when they make a relationship visible: the geography around Cuervo, the reach of its trade, the different resolution of two statistical series, or the coexistence of withdrawal and continued use. The existing source plates already let readers inspect many of the decisive documents. Further illustration does not require another general acquisition exercise.

**Length decision:** The author now prioritizes content over length. The earlier 40,000-60,000-word range is historical planning guidance, not a minimum to reach. This pass keeps the 31,494-word main text intact. It adds three figures and replaces one, taking the illustrated positions from 25 to 28.

## Sources and reproducibility

All four figures use evidence already held. The map dissolves 902 held 2020 census block polygons into the county outline. Place points come from the held 2020 DHC geography; Cuervo uses the regional audit's explicitly defined reference point. This is a locator, not a reconstruction of historical municipal or service boundaries. The map contains no invented road or railway route.

The trade diagram draws on six identified observations: N18, N08, N07, N10, N13 and N17 in the trade-network transcription. Solid and dashed arrows distinguish contemporary newspaper reports from Grubbs's retrospective reconstruction. The trade statement does not assign every shipment to Bond & Wiest. Outside suppliers remain unnamed.

The price figure checks all 66 held annual observations against nominal prices and CPI, then indexes real prices to 1951 = 100. Its five population observations agree with the Chapter 5 calculations. No annual population values, post-1950 counts, correlations or regression coefficients are invented. The different geographies, changing precinct definitions and missing later observations appear in the figure and caption.

## Verification and remaining design work

All 174 pages were rendered and inspected in 22 overview sheets; the four new figure pages were also inspected at 125 dpi. Checks confirm all body paragraphs, 220 note links, 174 note destinations, eight chapter bookmarks and 27 numbered figures (plus the unnumbered opening photograph). No clipping, overlap, caption spill or unplanned short text page remains. Revision 2's PDF hash is unchanged. Full results are recorded in the build manifest and validation file. Publication apparatus, final image reproduction and rights review, the complete photographic sequence, facing-page design and a physical proof remain later production tasks. These figures improve the reading proof without declaring those tasks complete.

The next useful visual decision is the photographic sequence: where the book needs spatial or material evidence that these charts cannot supply. More graphs should answer a specific new question before they are added.
