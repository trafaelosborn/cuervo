## Air conditioning

Its absence from draft 2 was not the result of an assessment finding it irrelevant. Targeted searches found no developed treatment in the current project notes. The user was asked to distinguish cars, buildings or both. Pending clarification, the stated working interpretation was car air conditioning.

Draft 3 introduces automotive cooling in the transport discussion. It separates the documented spread of the equipment from a possible change in stopping behavior. ASHRAE records 54% of new cars equipped in 1969. That figure does not describe the whole fleet or traffic through Cuervo. The suggestion that cooling could make longer journeys or fewer heat-relief stops attractive is identified as an inference. Additional travel could also create new potential customers. No local effect or particular closure is attributed to air conditioning.

The fuller discussion belongs in Chapter 6. Building cooling and regional migration are distinct questions; neither is silently equated with in-car cooling. The bounded assessment records sources, coverage and limits. No general acquisition gate has been added.
