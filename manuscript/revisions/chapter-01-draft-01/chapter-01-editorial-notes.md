## Editorial decisions

The user requested Chapter 1. This implements **“Reading Cuervo”** in the eight-chapter architecture, rather than the previously suggested Chapter 4 as a representative chapter. The main text is approximately 3,400 words, within Chapter 1's 3,000–4,500-word allocation. Fifteen working notes are separate from the main-text count.

The opening uses the author's actual recollections of dogs playing and an informal conversation about the church. No dialogue, precise date, weather, journey route or named interlocutor has been invented. The first-person reflection is proposed authorial prose for review. Later sections introduce the familiar highway account, the 1911 juxtaposition of investment and withdrawal, overlapping geographies, and the economic questions guiding the book.

The chapter introduces selected findings without reproducing the whole analytical report. Longer treatments of merchant finance, services, demographic change and transport remain for their allocated chapters. Its ending leads toward formation in Chapter 2. The formal theory discussion remains modest in this opening; later chapters must do the detailed explanatory work.

## Author review items

These are revision items for the concrete draft, not prerequisites that prevented completing it.
