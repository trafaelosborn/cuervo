## What the conclusion does

*What remained* returns to present ranching, the continuing geography of property, church affiliation and selected family connections. These observations distinguish a smaller town economy from an unused locality. The photographic discussion separates visible condition from evidence of dates and causes. The ending brings formation, contraction and regional reorganization together without sentimental narration or an invented terminal event.

The final sentence is: “The place continued to be used.”
