## What this chapter does

Brings the historical findings into an explicit economic explanation of cumulative contraction. Smith clarifies the market beyond village residents; linked contracts explain the merchant's organization; North separates export production from local diversification. Central-place theory clarifies overlapping service areas. Krugman supplies a conditional feedback framework, with the model's distance from Cuervo explicit.

The chapter develops different requirements for maintaining services, continuation versus reopening, possible coordination problems, uneven adaptation and the difference between local activity and residents' access. It tests the highway, agricultural disappearance, absorption, demographic and cooling shortcuts. It concludes with the distribution of gains and losses, leading into Chapter 8, *What remained*.
