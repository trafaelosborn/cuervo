# December 1, 1922 Clipper editorial: reading note

Source: `sources/02_newspapers/cuervo_clipper/full_scans/The Cuervo Clipper, 12-01-1922 - The Cuervo Clipper 12-01-1922.pdf`, PDF page 2, newspaper front page, columns 4-5. The repository cover is PDF page 1. Title: **To My Clipper Readers**. Signature: **Katharine A. Hicks**.

## Selected passages, visually transcribed

Line-end hyphenation is joined and paragraph spacing normalized. These are excerpts, not a complete diplomatic transcription. No modern interpretation is inserted into the quoted passages.

> It is with poignant regret that I announce my withdrawal from all financial connection with our little Cuervo paper. There are many reasons for my so doing. Chief among them is my conviction that under present economic conditions it is not possible for the people of Guadalupe County to support more than one first class weekly paper, and I believe that one should be published at the County seat.

> There can be a definite space allotted to each town or neighborhood of reasonable size, in which local happenings and advertisements may be printed, and in this way I believe a County paper can better serve the people, with more fairness to our merchants who support it, than can a number of small local sheets. This will probably not always be true when the country has become more thickly settled; but it is so now.

> I would like very much to establish such a paper as I have in mind, in this County, one equally as good as is the Tucumcari News, for instance, but my Clipper associate, Mr. Chas. Gerhardt, owns, and is now publishing, a democratic paper in Santa Rosa; and as he has decided to enlarge and improve it, he is in my opinion the one to undertake this task.

> It is his intention when he has completed his teaching contract, to set definitely about the improvements planned for in his Public Voice, and he has my promise of assistance when that time arrives. Meantime I expect to continue contributing to his paper, and so still keep in touch with my Clipper readers.

> Remember, friends, I am not quitting. I am merely passing on to Mr. Gerhardt the responsibility I am unable, as at present situated, to properly discharge. The main purpose of our purchase of the Cuervo Clipper was to bring about its return into Democratic ranks, and to use it to aid in the election of our democratic county and state tickets. These two main purposes have been accomplished. Our considerable financial loss Mr. Hicks and I are simply charging to our campaign contribution.

## Interpretation controls

- Hicks is an interested owner/investor and political participant, not a neutral surveyor of the county economy.
- The article states a financial withdrawal, advocates support for a county-seat paper, and records financial loss. It does not state an audited loss amount, dates for all losses, or an exact last-publication date.
- It names the Santa Rosa paper **Public Voice**, not Democratic Voice.
- The teaching contract belongs to Gerhardt. The article does not supply a health explanation involving Mr. Hicks.
- The same page carries Cuervo-located Armijo Mercantile and Bond-West advertisements; Bond-West's advertisement says “BUY AT HOME.” These are observations on this date, not opening/closure dates.
- Santa Rosa Mercantile Co.'s business name must not be used alone to geolocate its outlet.
- Only this editorial and surrounding front-page material were closely read for this report. The entire 267-issue collection has not been searched or transcribed.
