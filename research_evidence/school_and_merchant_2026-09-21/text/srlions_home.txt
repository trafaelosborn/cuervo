Welcome to Santa Rosa Consolidated Schools —


A Shared Vision


 


The primary purpose of Santa Rosa Consolidated Schools is to provide all students with the best academic opportunities leading to college career readiness and/or post in a wholesome, safe, twenty-first-century environment.


 


Our Literacy Vision/Goal


“SRCS is Cultivating Confident Readers and Writers Through Student-Centered, High-Quality Literacy Instruction.”


 


At SRCS, we are dedicated to cultivating confident, empowered readers and writers through purposeful, student-centered learning. Our goal is to foster a lifelong love of reading and writing by giving students voice, choice, and ownership in their learning journey. With high-quality, research-based instruction, we ensure every student experiences growth, success, and joy in literacy.

Why Santa Rosa Consolidated Schools?


 


The primary purpose of Santa Rosa Consolidated Schools is to provide all students with the best academic opportunities leading to college career readiness and/or post in a wholesome, safe, twenty-first-century environment.


S.R.C.S.’s proven approach to teaching helps teachers teach and students learn. It begins with our traditional philosophy of education. This teacher-directed framework empowers teachers to focus students’ attention on the material they need to learn—in a context and sequence that will help them learn it best.
