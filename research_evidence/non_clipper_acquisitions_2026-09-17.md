## Census: the most useful new evidence

The 1970 New Mexico report, **Table 10, printed page 33-14 (PDF page 24)**, reports:

| Geographic unit | 1960 | 1970 | Published change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Guadalupe County | 5,610 | 4,969 | -11.4% |
| Dilia division | 928 | 725 | -21.9% |
| Santa Rosa division | 3,380 | 3,221 | -4.7% |
| Santa Rosa municipality | 2,220 | 2,483 | +11.9% |
| Vaughn division | 1,302 | 1,023 | -21.4% |
| Vaughn municipality | 1,170 | 867 | -25.9% |

The 1960 report, **Table 7, printed page 33-8 (PDF page 9)**, independently confirms the 1960 entries. Santa Rosa appears as a town in 1960 and a city in 1970; Vaughn changes from village to town. The 1970 table's Guadalupe footnote, printed p. 33-16 (PDF p. 26), records those designation changes. It does not report a Guadalupe annexation in that note; a full boundary comparison remains separate work.

**What this supports:** Santa Rosa's municipal population rose during 1960-1970 while its surrounding census division and Guadalupe County declined. This gives a concrete comparison for the research question. These counts alone do not establish why populations changed or isolate an interstate effect.

**What remains unresolved:** neither cited table separately lists Cuervo. These reports use census county divisions (CCDs), whereas the earlier Cuervo series uses a precinct. Do not assign Santa Rosa division's total to Cuervo or append CCD totals to that precinct series. Boundary maps and smaller-area records are still needed. Published municipal counts also are not a direct measure of commercial activity.

The 1960 scans yielded little usable embedded text. Read their page images rather than relying on text search. The accompanying introduction/corrections PDF is retained for definitions and later verification; no assertion is made that all corrections have been reconciled.

[Local file](../sources/06_maps/usgs_newkirk_nm_1964_24000_geopdf.pdf) · [Repository record](https://tnmaccess.nationalmap.gov/api/v1/products?datasets=Historical%20Topographic%20Maps&bbox=-104.45,34.98,-104.37,35.05&max=100) · [Source download](https://prd-tnm.s3.amazonaws.com/StagedProducts/Maps/HistoricalTopo/PDF/NM/24000/NM_Newkirk_191674_1964_24000_geo.pdf)

Printed 1964; aerial photography 1963, field checked 1964. Detailed 1:24,000 map of Newkirk and the transport corridor east of Cuervo. It is not a pre-interstate baseline.



The two Santa Fe regional sheets extend our coverage before the existing 1963 Cuervo quadrangle, but are too small in scale for dependable building counts. The sheet named 1954 actually contains a later revision than the sheet cataloged 1958. No earlier detailed Cuervo 1:24,000 sheet was located in this catalog query; that is not proof none exists elsewhere. None of these map dates alone resolves an exact highway opening date.
