## What is still unproved

The accumulated sources show population and institutional patterns worth investigating. They do not yet demonstrate that Cuervo's departing residents or businesses moved to Santa Rosa, or isolate highway effects from farming, rail service, water, public investment, county-seat functions, or school decisions. The next research needs matched evidence for both settlements over time, including evidence that might contradict the proposed explanation.

For further archive acquisition, the strongest starting targets are the **town-company book, Bond & Wiest accounts, school records, and highway/land files**. For work that can start immediately, the strongest targets are **Clipper indexing and census transcription**.
