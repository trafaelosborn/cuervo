## Findings with immediate manuscript uses

### 1. A Cuervo-specific map of the merchant and railway arrangement

[City of Cuervo map](<B:/Meridian/Cuervo/Cuervo/Cuervo/Parcel Map/City of Cuervo.pdf>), one scanned sheet, shows the original townsite and Gonzales, Gross Kelly and Park additions. In the railway corridor the printed base labels the depot, a section house and the Bond & Wiest warehouse. The warehouse lies immediately beside the drawn railway; a Bond & Wiest store label is partly obscured by later coloring.

**Use:** a candidate Chapter 2 illustration explaining the spatial relationship between merchant facilities, transport and the lot grid. The drawing makes a historical arrangement easier to understand than a generic railroad image.

**Dating limit:** no secure date for the base map or ownership annotations was established in this inspection. They are distinct layers. Do not describe all named uses and colored ownerships as contemporaneous. Platted lots do not establish occupied buildings, population or realized development. The scan's title is not evidence of municipal incorporation. Preserve the original and label any editorial redraw and its inferred elements.

### 2. The school parcel and a specific chain of record references

[C-232 school survey](<B:/Meridian/Cuervo/Cuervo/Cuervo/Cuervo Plats/C-232.pdf>), dated December 21, 2010 and filed December 27, maps Block 15 of the Gross, Kelly & Co. addition, **2.0661 acres**, and the footprint of the District No. 6 masonry-stone school. It was prepared for Santa Rosa Consolidated Schools. The survey cites:

- Gross Kelly to school: Book 6, page 56, filed March 25, 1913.
- Gross Kelly to the board of education: Book 36, page 394, August 1943.
- Board of education to Gross Kelly: Book 34, page 294.

The [2026 Gross Kelly subdivision report](<B:/Meridian/Cuervo/Cuervo/Cuervo/CuervoOwnerReport/GrossKellyAddition.txt>), printed page 4, describes the 1943 deed as an exchange for Block 10. It records a description error in the return deed, filed October 13, 1943. This is the assessor's account of those instruments; the deeds themselves have not been inspected.

**Use:** a concrete school-site illustration and a short, bounded historical lead for Chapter 4. Relate this carefully to the 1910-1912 newspaper building sequence already held. A later survey and deed references do not independently establish that the surviving building is the same structure, or that a 1943 land transaction entailed moving the school.

### 3. A commercial building described on a salvage basis

[Townsite card packet ending 144438405](<B:/Meridian/Cuervo/Cuervo/Cuervo/Cuervo Townsite/scan_20260922144438405.pdf>), pages 1-2, concerns **original-townsite Block 20, lots 8-12**. Page 2 checks gas station under type/use, marks poor physical condition, includes a measured footprint and repeatedly uses salvage terminology. The preparation entries at the foot read January 1983; a clear later annotation dated December 15, 2008 says no change.

**Use:** evidence of recorded physical condition and continued assessment of commercial fabric. It supplies a parcel identity for linking an image or other record.

**Limit:** this does not identify the operator or last trading date. The card's effective age is an appraisal field, not a documented construction date. Its valuation is not a sale price. No causal claim about I-40 follows from this observation alone.

### 4. Later geography and consolidation can be located precisely

The three [C-286 sheets](<B:/Meridian/Cuervo/Cuervo/Cuervo/Cuervo Plats/C-286(1).pdf>) describe Tract I of a Page and Lindsey/Jones land-exchange agreement, dated August 2013. Sheets 2 and 3 map its parcels and improvements. The cover notes distinguish the platted grid from uncertain dedication and roadway records; this is the surveyor's stated limitation, not a legal conclusion reached here.

[C-292(2)](<B:/Meridian/Cuervo/Cuervo/Cuervo/Cuervo Plats/C-292(2).pdf>), July 2014, is explicitly sheet 2 of 5 for Tract II. It shows portions of the townsite and additions in relation to I-40 and the railway. The other four sheets of that set are not present under matching C-292 names in this packet.

**Use:** modern spatial cross-reference and evidence that the old lot grid persisted in later land transactions. The reports are not a continuous historical assessment series. Do not infer timing or motives of consolidation from present ownership alone, and do not add overlapping report-summary totals as though they were distinct properties.

## Other observations and limits

- The 2026 reports are explicitly dated modern snapshots. Their legal-description notes can preserve earlier references; their current categories do not directly establish historical use, actual occupancy or present livestock activity.
- One sampled residential card records a 1960 construction year and later maintenance annotations. Another notes mobile homes, one described as an old post office, in December 2008. These are useful bounded observations, not a village-wide chronology.
- Some scans are faint or rotated; at least one first page is essentially dark. Transcribe handwriting against the image rather than treating OCR as authoritative.
- The supplied retention chart is contextual evidence of the document provided. No conclusion about current legal requirements or actual destruction of particular records is drawn from it.
