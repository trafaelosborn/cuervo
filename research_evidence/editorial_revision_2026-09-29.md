## Air conditioning

Sylvain Leduc and Daniel J. Wilson, [“Snow Belt to Sun Belt Migration: End of an Era?”](https://www.frbsf.org/wp-content/uploads/wp2024-21.pdf), Federal Reserve Bank of San Francisco Working Paper 2024-21 (July 2024), pp. 1–2: acceleration toward the South and Southwest after the 1960s and cooling as a likely contributor. This does not isolate a cooling treatment effect or examine Cuervo–Santa Rosa movement.

Edward L. Glaeser and Kristina Tobio, [“The Rise of the Sunbelt”](https://www.nber.org/papers/w13071), NBER Working Paper 13071 (2007), pp. 2–5: alternative roles for productivity, amenities and housing supply. The held paper is at `sources/home_cooling_2026-09-24/glaeser_tobio_2007_sunbelt.pdf`. These sources motivate a plausible regional mechanism and its debated importance; the earlier timing and missing local contrasts limit the Cuervo application. Santa Rosa's AC prevalence and population change do not identify the population path without cooling.

The revision retains the primary works already used in the project, with their fit stated more narrowly: Smith on market extent; North on export income, its disposition and ancillary work; Braverman–Stiglitz on linked contracts; Hsu–Holmes–Morgan on an optimal hierarchy under model assumptions; Krugman on manufacturing-location feedback; Glaeser–Gottlieb on earnings, housing and amenities.
