## Additional observation: December 1922 health conferences

The December 1 front page's Health Conference notice announces Santa Rosa on December 4, Pintada on December 6, Cuervo on December 13 and Newkirk on December 15. It states that conferences will be held in school buildings, 10 a.m.-4 p.m. Children below school age are to be weighed, measured and examined.

The chapter uses this as evidence of a scheduled regional arrangement and intended use of school buildings. It does **not** report observed attendance, completed examinations, subsequent health outcomes or a continuously available general practice. The nurse's name and program funding were not needed for the passage and are not supplied from uncertain print. The nearby vaccination notice is not used.
