# Cuervo: passenger stops, through trains, and freight operations

*September 19, 2026. New timetable checks and current-route verification.*

**September 20 correction:** newly acquired originals show Cuervo stops on the Imperial in 1955–1958. The Rock Island timetable effective April 27, 1958 still advertises stops; the SP operating timetable effective October 26, 1958 no longer marks them. This supersedes the 1955 lead and the wider 1954–1960 bracket below. See the [new local-functions report](rail_local_functions_2026-09-20.md), including the separate office, freight-rights and stockyard questions.

## Answer supported so far

**The line through the Cuervo corridor remains an active freight route. Through passenger service ended with the Golden State in 1968. Cuervo's own scheduled passenger service changed earlier: the latest positive and first negative timetables now inspected bracket the change between April 27 and October 26, 1958. The exact last boarding date remains unverified, as do the local freight and station-payroll end dates.**

This distinguishes three events that should not be assigned one date: losing a passenger stop; ending the corridor's passenger trains; and withdrawing local freight handling or station staff. A further event, abandonment of the through railway itself, is contradicted by the current freight network.

The 1954 and 1960 route tables were read from rendered originals in both directions. The January 1955 condensed route pages were also visually checked. The 1968 Congressional Record page was visually checked; its printed date in the discontinuance passage reads March 25, whereas its embedded OCR reads March 26. Search text and derivative accounts also disagree about February and April last-run dates. These differences are preserved rather than silently resolved.

## Superseded working lead: 1955

The secondary [Californian train history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Californian_%28train%29), citing Lucius Beebe's 1963 railroad history, says that successor trains 43/44 last ran January 9, 1955. This was a lead for the trains identified serving Cuervo in 1954, not a verified local closure. **The September 20 check resolves that distinction:** the full SP employee timetable effective January 9, 1955 shows an eastbound regular Cuervo stop on Imperial 40 and a westbound flag stop on Imperial 39. Later public timetables continue to advertise Cuervo service through April 27, 1958. January 9, 1955 must not be used as the end of Cuervo passenger access.

## Implication for the project

Cuervo had an advertised passenger stop after the documented 1952 road relocation. By the 1960 timetable that local access is no longer shown. This makes the 1950s a useful interval for examining the combined effects of road access, cattle conditions, and local railway functions. The late date of the through train's 1968 disappearance cannot explain the earlier population decline by itself.

The town could lose local economic benefits while through trains continued to pass. The remaining questions are when cattle loading or other local freight handling changed, when an agency station was downgraded or closed, and when locally based railway employment changed. The 1950 census has seven working residents with railway occupations plus two people reporting previous railway jobs while looking for work; residence does not establish the workplace. Those observations should be linked to station and personnel records rather than assigned the passenger train's discontinuance date.
