# Cuervo sources we can use online

Checked September 16, 2026. This inventory distinguishes downloaded historical material from catalog descriptions and unresolved access problems.

## New downloads verified today

### Cuervo Clipper, February 4, 1910: one scanned page

- [Public UNM PDF](https://econtent.unm.edu/digital/api/collection/dignews/id/2504/download)
- [Local research copy](../sources/02_newspapers/cuervo_clipper/full_scans/1910-02-04_unm-dignews-2504.pdf)
- [Embedded OCR text](../sources/02_newspapers/cuervo_clipper/full_scans/1910-02-04_unm-dignews-2504_ocr.txt)
- **Verified:** a valid, unencrypted, one-page PDF, 446,188 bytes. Rendered and visually inspected. The masthead reads February 4, 1910, Volume 2, No. 44. A page number is not established. This is not represented as the complete issue.

Useful evidence visible on this page:

1. A report of a new telephone connection with Montoya and other points.
2. A report that the Long Commission Company planned to convert the livery barn to a garage. This establishes a reported plan, not completion. The page also still carries a T. L. Lewis livery advertisement, showing why ads and news must be compared carefully.
3. Advertisements for Bond & Wiest and the Cuervo Drug Store, plus a Santa Rosa bank soliciting business from the paper's readers.
4. An E. P. & S. W. passenger-train notice with Cuervo departure times and designated stops.
5. Local property-sale news and the formation of an Odd Fellows lodge.

Interpretation: this page supplies names, businesses, and changes to investigate. It shows Santa Rosa marketing services to Cuervo readers well before I-40; it does not by itself measure the extent of Santa Rosa's economic influence or establish a cause of Cuervo's decline. OCR misreads columns and characters; use the image for quotations and train times.

**Access limit:** the exact public PDF URL above downloads successfully, but its older `dignews` collection viewer currently reports that the collection is unavailable/restricted. I did not enumerate that restricted collection. This one public file does not establish access to the entire newspaper run.

### William J. Lucas: Cuervo, March 1986

- [UNM item and metadata](https://econtent.unm.edu/digital/collection/wjlr66/id/9/rec/1)
- [Local access image](../sources/09_photographs/lucas_cuervo_1986-03_986-012-0009_access.jpg)
- Identifier **986-012-0009.tif**; collection **PICT-986-012**.
- Saved the publicly displayed JPEG, **639 × 441 pixels**, 45,834 bytes; this is an access image, not the original TIFF or a publication master. The interface also exposes a **Download → Medium** option.
- Visually checked: the photograph shows a building bearing U.S. Post Office lettering and adjacent structures. The lettering alone does not establish that a post office was operating on the photograph date.
- The item date comes from UNM's metadata. The item and collection finding aid have differing rights statements; seek clarification for publication use.

## Cataloged records still requiring an access inquiry

I did not locate public scans of the Cuervo Town Company record book, Bond & Wiest business ledgers, the county school folders, Cuervo Telephone Co. file, or Morris congressional post-office folder. The exact catalog references remain useful for ordering reproductions or arranging visits; a downloadable finding-aid PDF would only reproduce the description.

See [the catalog pull list](archives_catalog_pull_list_2026-09-16.md) for repositories and locations. This is a report of what was found, not proof that no digitized copies exist elsewhere.
