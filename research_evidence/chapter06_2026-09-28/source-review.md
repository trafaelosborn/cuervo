## Interpretive choices

- The opening uses a documented transport interruption to explain the difference between passing traffic and local custom.
- Passenger stops, train-order offices, freight agency/accounting, loading facilities, actual shipments and through service remain separate variables.
- Santa Rosa's commercial concentration and its railway billing assignment to Tucumcari demonstrate that regional hierarchies differed by function.
- General economic explanations of trip costs, combined errands, supply channels and local expenditure are presented as mechanisms. They are not estimated causal effects.
- The Santa Rosa comparison uses the reported 1972 chronology and corrected census count. Its 1960-1970 gain is not presented as post-bypass growth.
- The main text contains no invented closing dates, precise last cattle shipment, migration rate, universal survival threshold or causal shares.
- Chapter 7 retains responsibility for sustained engagement with canonical economic literature. This chapter develops the historical arrangements that synthesis must explain.
