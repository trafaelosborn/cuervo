## Interpretation

Production, ownership, affiliation and physical survival are distinct observations. They support continuing use without making loss inconsequential or implying restoration of the former town economy. The conclusion explains the changing need to concentrate work at Cuervo. It does not prescribe redevelopment or infer present residents' preferences.
