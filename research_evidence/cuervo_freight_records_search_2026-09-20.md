# Cuervo freight records: follow-up search

*September 20, 2026. Follows the [1974 freight-accounting study](cuervo_freight_accounting_1974_2026-09-20.md).*

## Result

**No actual post-1958 Cuervo cattle shipment has been located in this search.** The strongest new lead is a cataloged group of car-movement microfiche at the University of Oklahoma. An earlier freight-station directory survives at Illinois, and photographs establish a September 1967 Rock Island station-book edition. Neither directory's Cuervo entry has been obtained.

Seven additional PDFs, totaling 320 pages, and one source-index HTML file were downloaded. Most are contextual sources or finding aids. The shipment records themselves remain unacquired. This result does not change the previously established 1958 passenger-stop bracket or the 1974 freight-accounting observation.

## 1. Better targets for actual movements and facilities

The [Rock Island Technical Society finding aid](https://lib.ou.edu/sites/default/files/RockIslandTechnicalSociety.pdf), collection **WHC-M-2534**, supplies these targets. Relevant complete pages 1–3 were read visually.

| Location | Cataloged material | What still needs checking |
|---|---|---|
| **Box 1**, PDF 1 | Car-movement microfiche, **1976–83**; track charts, 1973 | Whether the fiche includes Cuervo origins/destinations, commodities, dates and car identifiers; how it is indexed. The date span alone does not establish continuous service. |
| **Box 14**, PDF 2 | Revenue by car type, 1978; revenue by district, 1979 | Whether any station-level breakdown exists. Aggregate revenue cannot be assigned to Cuervo. |
| **Boxes 29–30**, PDF 3 | Side-track records | Geographic coverage and whether they document a loading track, lease, retirement or removal at Cuervo. No Cuervo-specific item is cataloged here. |

[Held finding aid](../sources/01_railroad/local_functions_2026-09-20/ou_rock_island_technical_society_finding_aid.pdf). These are descriptions of physical holdings, not digitized car-movement records.

The corporate collection, **WHC-M-703**, remains useful alongside this new collection: **Box 480** includes waybill reports dated 1966–1984; **Box 157** contains a 1979 Station Master ledger. The exact descriptions, page references and other corporate targets are retained in the [previous study](cuervo_freight_accounting_1974_2026-09-20.md). No newly found evidence confirms their Cuervo coverage.

The current records offer an availability contact list through **specialresearchcollections@ou.edu**. No reopening date was given on the records inspected September 20.

This dated restriction takes precedence over the generic public-access language also present in the catalog. Online finding aids remain readable, but immediate access to the underlying boxes cannot be assumed. No inquiry or subscription was submitted. ## 2. Earlier station-directory leads

### A 1958 freight directory at Illinois

The University of Illinois' **Maynard J. Brichford Railroad Ephemera Collection, IHLC MS 910**, lists an **Official List of Open and Prepay Stations, 1958**, in **Box 2**. This is printed page 2 / PDF 3 of the [official inventory](https://www.library.illinois.edu/ihx/inventories/brichford-railroad.pdf), read visually and [downloaded](../sources/01_railroad/local_functions_2026-09-20/illinois_brichford_railroad_finding_aid.pdf).

This is a specific physical-volume lead near the passenger-service change. Its Cuervo entry, edition month, explanatory symbols, restrictions and carrier notes remain unseen. It might establish published freight status; it would not by itself establish an actual cattle shipment or usable stock pens. The finding aid gives **ihlc@library.illinois.edu** as the collection contact. No request has been sent and current retrieval availability has not been checked.

### September 1967 Rock Island list

An [eBay listing with nine photographs](https://www.ebay.com/itm/206139269786) shows a Rock Island *List of Stations*. **September 1967** is legible on the contents-page photograph, independently of the seller's title. The listing's item-specific field gives a conflicting 1954 date; the printed date is the basis used here.

The photographed contents identify the Southern Division on pages 27–39, a numerical station list on 57–65 and an alphabetical index on 66–78. Photographs of Southern Division pages 27 and 35 cover other territory; the last photograph is Illinois Division page 1. **No Cuervo entry or billing arrangement was recovered from the photographs.** The full book has not been purchased or downloaded. This establishes an earlier edition to seek, not an earlier date for Tucumcari billing.

## 3. Freight manuals: useful regional context, no Cuervo movement

Two original Rock Island manuals were downloaded from [Moving the Freight](https://movingthefreight.com/railroads/rock-island/).

| Held source | Readings and limits |
|---|---|
| [1972 freight schedules and blocking instructions](../sources/01_railroad/local_functions_2026-09-20/ri_freight_schedules_1972.pdf), 110 PDF pages | Cover dated August 1, 1972; the scan contains later revisions, including 1973/1974 material. Printed 146–147 / PDF 108 describes Amarillo–Tucumcari local service and an as-needed train 741. Printed 147 is revised October 3, 1972. **This territory is east of Tucumcari; Cuervo lies west.** It corroborates the existence of as-needed regional freight operations without establishing Cuervo pickup. |
| [Operations manual hosted as March 1, 1979](../sources/01_railroad/local_functions_2026-09-20/ri_freight_schedules_1979.pdf), 97 PDF pages | Host filename supplies the issue date; inspected local-service pages include late-1978 revision dates. The index and local-service contact sheets were reviewed, with PDF 90 read at full-page scale. No Cuervo shipment or local pickup was identified in that bounded review. |

Both scans are image-only. Empty text extraction is not an absence finding. Local-service sections, indexes and selected complete pages were inspected; neither manual was exhaustively transcribed. Through-train schedules at Tucumcari cannot answer whether animals were loaded at Cuervo.

A separately downloaded [1952–1973 train-consist compilation](../sources/01_railroad/local_functions_2026-09-20/ri_consists_1952_1973.pdf) contains normal **passenger** train makeup. It is not a freight manifest or a record of dated cattle movements.

## 4. Stockyard maps acquired

[WX4's Tucumcari directory](https://www.wx4.org/to/foam/sp/southwest/carrizozo_sub/a_directory.html) links Shasta Division Archives copies of east- and west-end station engineering maps. Both original PDFs are held:

- [East end](../sources/01_railroad/local_functions_2026-09-20/tucumcari_station_map_1929_east.pdf): the inspected title block is dated **December 26, 1929**, although the hosting filename says September 18. Later correction notation is not sufficiently legible to assign an exact date. The drawing labels stock pens and a hay barn and shows numbered pens and chutes next to railway tracks.
- [West end](../sources/01_railroad/local_functions_2026-09-20/tucumcari_station_map_1929_west.pdf): the overview shows the western yard, sidings and industrial facilities. Exact title-block dating was not independently transcribed in this pass.

These are **Tucumcari** facilities in an earlier period. They do not locate Cuervo's pens, prove post-1958 use, or show that a Cuervo rancher took animals to Tucumcari. Their value is regional physical context. Full-sheet overviews and the east-end detail are retained in the evidence folder.

## 5. Search coverage and unresolved leads

- Indexed web searches combined Cuervo/Tucumcari with cattle, stock cars, shipments, car orders, waybills, station traffic, station books and the 1974 identifiers. None yielded a verified post-1958 Cuervo livestock transaction.
- OU's current ArchivesSpace keyword search returned no Cuervo record. Its 14 Tucumcari results were checked across two result pages; they comprised a subject entry and photographs/postcards rather than named freight records. Numerous boxes are described only in finding aids, so this catalog result cannot exclude relevant material.
- The current corporate catalog links the already held boxes 1–517 finding aid. The separate diagrams inventory mentioned in that aid was not located. RITS' eight-item oversized list supplies no named Cuervo drawing.
- The [University of Iowa Vander Maas inventory](https://www.lib.uiowa.edu/scua/msc/tomsc500/msc468/multipleseries.html) lists Rock Island officer/station-agent lists for 1902–1919 in Box 29, folder 5. These are an early staffing lead, not a post-1958 freight record; no scans were acquired.
- The [February 1985 government-publications catalog](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-GP3-6b7a0f54f5f81c21330a46ccccaa9034/pdf/GOVPUB-GP3-6b7a0f54f5f81c21330a46ccccaa9034.pdf) supplied a search-result bibliographic lead to the December 21, 1979 final environmental impact statement for the Rock Island purchase proceedings: SuDoc **IC 1.32:Sa 21/final**, OCLC **11359460**, Finance Docket 28799 and related proceedings. The actual statement was not acquired, so no Cuervo conclusion is drawn from it.

## 6. What would settle the question

The next useful record is a dated car order, waybill or station return identifying **both the commodity and the actual loading origin**. The 1974 search keys are **Cuervo: car-accounting 16178; SPLC 774115; revenue/audit 8355; billing Tucumcari 8310**. Earlier identifiers may differ.

A Tucumcari billing entry needs a separate origin check. A Cuervo cattle movement after 1958 would establish continued use at that date, but even the latest located movement would not automatically establish the final shipment. Dating closure requires a discontinuance notice, tariff change, facility retirement or a sufficiently complete run of traffic records.

The useful distinction remains: local cattle loading could continue after passenger service and local staffing changed. Existing documents establish the administrative mechanism and 1974 accounting identity. The transaction evidence is still missing.
