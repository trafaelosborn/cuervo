## Verified acquisition routes

**State Archives:** [official instructions](https://www.srca.nm.gov/nm-archives-heritage-search/) request the catalog citations on their form, emailed to archives@srca.nm.gov. Research appointments are listed Tuesday-Thursday, 10 a.m.-1 p.m.; remote inquiries Monday-Friday, 8 a.m.-5 p.m. A visit still needs arrangement. No appointment was booked.

**UNM:** [using CSWR collections](https://library.unm.edu/cswr/using-cswr-collections.php) says appointments and advance requests are not ordinarily required, staff retrieve the closed-stack materials, and two free self-service scanning stations are available; bring a USB drive. Book condition and dimensions may affect scanning. [Reproduction instructions](https://library.unm.edu/cswr/reproductions.php) offer fee-based staff duplication and link a [request form](https://unm.libwizard.com/f/cswr-duplication-request). No reproduction order or payment was made. The linked fee schedule is labeled 2017, so current costs must be confirmed before relying on it.

## Additional origins leads encountered

These were visible as catalog titles/links in the Bond collection organization. They have not been acquired or read, and the financial/correspondence units may concern other branches. They have not been added to a new external request.

- [Bond & Wiest invoices, 1903](https://nmarchives.unm.edu/repositories/22/archival_objects/152777): possible early purchasing evidence.
- [Bond & Wiest letters, copy, 1906-1909](https://nmarchives.unm.edu/repositories/22/archival_objects/152839): possible contemporary explanations of customers, credit, operations and expectations.
- [Bond Company letters, 1899-1902](https://nmarchives.unm.edu/repositories/22/archival_objects/152642): possible formation-period correspondence; Cuervo relevance unknown.
- [Bond Company P. & L. summaries, 1891-1916](https://nmarchives.unm.edu/repositories/22/archival_objects/152691): potential profit/loss evidence; branch coverage and accounting comparability unknown.
- [Loadings CRIP](https://nmarchives.unm.edu/repositories/22/archival_objects/152588): potentially relevant to the railway shipping question. Catalog displays the broad collection dates; actual dates, locations and contents unconfirmed.

An account ledger can establish customers and transactions without explaining the original investment decision. Correspondence and town-company minutes may supply that missing link. None of these uninspected titles establishes the town's founding date, the store's opening date or its profitability.
