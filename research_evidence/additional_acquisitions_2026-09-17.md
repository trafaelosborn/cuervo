Start with [Cuervo's first household sheet](../sources/03_census/1950_manuscripts/ed_10-6/43290879-New_Mexico-180130-0002.jpg). The county map uses a **1938 highway-map base with 1950 census annotations**. It visibly places Cuervo within a precinct extending well beyond the town. Use it to distinguish a precinct population from a town population; do not assume these boundaries apply unchanged to earlier decades. Its roads are not a fresh 1950 road survey. The description pages supply the selected districts' written limits, including Santa Rosa's internal division.

**Count discrepancy to retain for review:** Cuervo's portfolio cover has a field total of 210 and a Washington-office total of 211. Its ED description has a handwritten 210. The project's published-table series currently uses 210. These administrative counts have not been reconciled; the acquisition does not silently replace the published series.

## 4. 1930 population report

[Population Volume I, chapter file including New Mexico](../sources/03_census/us_census_1930_population_volume1_ch07_including_nm.pdf), 140 PDF pages. This download contains several states and begins with Nebraska.

- New Mexico's narrative begins on **PDF page 66, printed page 729**.
- **Guadalupe County: Table 4, PDF page 71, printed page 734.** Cuervo precinct is 287 in 1930 and 438 in 1920; Santa Rosa precinct and village are separate rows.
- Read the table's introduction on PDF page 69 and the Guadalupe boundary footnote on page 71. Blank earlier-year cells are not zeroes.

**Discrepancy flagged:** the 1930 volume's Santa Rosa village row prints **1,093 for 1920**, while the current project series records **1,003** from the 1920 source. Recheck the original 1920 scan before revising the series or its growth calculations. This acquisition note records the conflict without treating it as resolved.

Source: [Census Bureau, 1930 Population, Volume I](https://www.census.gov/library/publications/1931/dec/1930a-vol-01-population.html).

## 5. Retail business records: 1930 census, operations in 1929

Four PDFs downloaded. **Business reference year is 1929**, even though the census is called 1930 and the volume was published in 1933. Each chapter file contains more than just New Mexico.

| File | Length | Useful location |
|---|---:|---|
| [Part I, chapter 3](../sources/03_census/us_census_1930_retail_distribution_part1_ch03_including_nm.pdf) | 151 pages | Table 13, New Mexico: **PDF page 124, printed 166**. County and town store counts, personnel, payroll, stocks, sales. |
| [Part I, chapter 7](../sources/03_census/us_census_1930_retail_distribution_part1_ch07_including_nm.pdf) | 134 pages | Table 14, Guadalupe County and Santa Rosa: **PDF pages 17-18, printed 642-643**, rows 26-28. Read both halves of the table. |
| [Part III, chapter 4](../sources/03_census/us_census_1930_retail_distribution_part3_ch04_including_nm.pdf) | 146 pages | New Mexico starts **PDF page 123, printed 149**; contents on 124/150. Statewide business-type tables begin 125/151. |
| [Definitions and explanation of terms](../sources/03_census/us_census_1930_retail_distribution_definitions.pdf) | 12 pages | Business classifications and definitions for interpretation. |

### Initial visual reading of Table 13

The dollar columns are printed in **thousands of dollars**; the table below converts net sales to dollars. Counts reflect the census's retail coverage and definitions, rather than all kinds of businesses.

| Geography | Retail stores | Full-time employees | Net sales, 1929 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Guadalupe County, total | 67 | 109 | $1,415,000 |
| Santa Rosa | 23 | 48 | $629,000 |
| Balance of county | 44 | 61 | $786,000 |

Santa Rosa is included in the county total. The balance is **all of the county outside Santa Rosa**, not Cuervo alone. Table 14 supplies business-type counts; sales marked `(x)` are suppressed and must not be treated as zero. Table 13 separately lists incorporated places of at least 1,000 population, so it does not provide a separate Cuervo row.

These are useful observations before the 1930s divergence. One year's retail share does not demonstrate that business moved from Cuervo to Santa Rosa.

Source: [Census Bureau, 1930 Retail Distribution, Volume I](https://www.census.gov/library/publications/1933/dec/1930h-vol-01-distribution.html).

## 6. Rock Island's 1902 annual report, contemporary printing

[Commercial and Financial Chronicle, June 7, 1902](../sources/01_railroad/commercial_financial_chronicle_1902-06-07.pdf), 58 pages.

- **PDF page 43, printed 1201:** heading identifies Chicago Rock Island & Pacific Railway Company's twenty-second annual report, year ending March 31, 1902.
- **PDF page 44, printed 1202, left column, "El Paso Extension":** describes the connecting railways reaching Santa Rosa and onward to El Paso, and the through passenger and freight connection.

This is a contemporary printing of the company's report in the financial press. It is useful evidence for Santa Rosa's junction role. It does not by itself identify Cuervo's station-opening date. The standalone company-issued report remains unacquired.

Source: [FRASER, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis](https://fraser.stlouisfed.org/title/commercial-financial-chronicle-1339/june-7-1902-536307). The official IIIF manifest containing its download link is saved in the logs.
