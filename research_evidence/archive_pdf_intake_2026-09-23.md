The guide's Historical Information, pages 3-4, describes the Cuervo business as a smaller branch of a wider mercantile network that also engaged in sheep, wool, land and credit. It does not date the Cuervo branch's opening. Its account of family-level liquidation after Franklin Bond's death in 1953 does not establish a Cuervo store closure date. This is repository-authored collection context, not a reading of the original business records.

Page 6 identifies another research lead: Frank H. Grubbs, *Frank Bond, Gentleman Sheepherder of Northern New Mexico, 1883-1915*, unpublished UNM thesis, 1958. No thesis copy was obtained in this intake.
