# Cuervo

## An Economic History of a New Mexico Ghost Town

First private GitHub release of the October 21, 2026 monograph, assembled October 26, 2026.

The release contains the completed eight-chapter book, with 169 source notes, 18 photographs, and 148 US Letter pages, including an intentional final blank for duplex printing. The repository supplies chapter sources, research tables and transcriptions, figure assets, provenance records, and production scripts.

## Downloads

- **Print edition:** `cuervo-monograph-print-letter.pdf`, 42,086,440 bytes.

The two editions have identical text, pagination, links, and bookmarks. The compact edition reduces photographic detail at high magnification; charts retain their original pixels and archival scans retain their original pixel dimensions.

Print on US Letter paper, in color, at Actual size / 100%, double-sided with long-edge flipping: 74 sheets.
